Standard Operating Procedure: Pool Water Sampling & Testing

Happy Fish Swim School • Water Quality Monitoring • Rev 4.0

Free Chlorine (FC) Target

Normal range: 1.0 – 4.0 ppm (mg/L)
Subject to different pools and weekdays or weekends.
Formula: Total drops of DPD-D × 0.2

pH Level Target

Normal range: 7.2 – 7.8
Optimal range: 7.4 – 7.6

Part 1: Pool Water Sample Collection

▶ Watch: Pool Water Sample Collection

1

Rinse and Stir

Go to your branch's designated pool. Submerge and stir the jar 2 to 3 times to rinse the jar and mix the surrounding water. Empty the rinse water away from your sampling area.

2

Turn Jar Mouth Down

Hold the jar vertical with the open mouth pointing straight down toward the water surface.

3

Submerge to Elbow Depth

Push the inverted jar straight down into the pool until water reaches your elbow (approx. 30–45 cm). Keeping the opening facing down prevents surface oils and debris from entering.

4

Turn Upright to Fill

Turn the jar upright while still at elbow depth. Allow all air bubbles to rise out completely so the jar fills with deep, well-mixed water.

5

Retrieve Sample

Lift the jar smoothly out of the pool. Use this sample immediately for the Free Chlorine and pH tests below.

Part 2: Free Chlorine (FC) Testing Procedure

►
▶ Watch: New Test Kit FC Test

Click the image above to open and watch the video directly on YouTube.

1

Measure Water Sample

Take 20 mL of the collected water sample.

2

Add DPD-1 & DPD-2

Add 5 drops of DPD-1 and 5 drops of DPD-2 into the water sample. Mix together. The sample water color will turn to pink.

3

Titrate with DPD-D & Stir

Drip DPD-D into the sample. After each drop, stir with the spoon. Repeat this step drop by drop until the sample water turns completely transparent. Count the total number of drops used.

4

Calculate Free Chlorine Reading

Each drop of DPD-D represents 0.2 ppm.

Calculation Formula: Total Drops × 0.2 = Free Chlorine (ppm)
Normal Range: 1.0 – 4.0 ppm (mg/L), subject to different pools and weekdays or weekends.
Example: 10 drops × 0.2 = 2.0 ppm FC
5

Report to WhatsApp Group

Record the reading in your branch log sheet and notify the team:

Part 3: pH Testing Procedure

►
▶ Watch: New Test Kit pH Test

Click the image above to open and watch the video directly on YouTube.

1

Fill Water Sample to Black Line

Fill up water sample in the pH compartment until the black line.

2

Add pH Solution (Phenol Red)

Drip 5 drops of pH solution (Phenol Red) into the compartment.

3

Cap and Invert

Cap the tube and invert several times until the liquid turns a uniform shade ranging from yellow (acidic) to reddish-purple (alkaline).

4

Compare Color & Record

Compare the color against the pH color scale under natural daylight. Record the value into your daily log (Target: 7.2 – 7.8).

5

Report to WhatsApp Group

Record the pH reading in your log and report:

💬 Send the pH reading to the Pool Maintenance WhatsApp group immediately.

Key Reminders & Rules

  • Testing Schedule: Test twice daily — AM test (~9:00 AM) and PM test (~5:00 PM).
  • Standard Spot: Always collect from the assigned pool/spot listed above for consistency across all branches.
  • Avoid Inlets & Jets: Never collect samples directly beside return jets, chemical dosing points, or skimmer boxes.
  • Cleanliness: Rinse the measuring cylinder and spoon with clean tap water after completing the tests.
